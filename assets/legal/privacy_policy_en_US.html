Notice: This is demo copy only. Before release, replace and approve it with Legal, Information Security, and the data protection owner based on actual processing activities.

MERP (the “App”) values the security of your personal information and enterprise data. This policy explains how the App handles information relating to you.

1. Scope

This policy applies when you use the MERP mobile app, sign-in services, feature menu, and connected enterprise services.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account information: account, name, organization, role, and sign-in state;
  • Security information: verification codes, device identifiers, sign-in time, network and error logs;
  • Business information: enterprise business data you submit, view, or process through authorized features;
  • Device permissions: requested only when you actively use functions such as location or camera.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, provide enterprise functions, maintain operational security, diagnose issues, and meet legal or enterprise management obligations.

4. Storage and protection

Credentials and security settings may be stored in secure device storage and enterprise services as needed. We use reasonable measures such as access control, encrypted transmission, and audit logging.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not share personal information with unrelated third parties unless necessary authorization is obtained, a legal obligation applies, or it is required to provide the enterprise service you use. Service providers must meet corresponding data protection obligations.

6. Your rights

You may contact your enterprise administrator or designated contact channel to inquire about, correct, or request handling of information relating to you. If a request cannot be completed immediately because of account permissions or retention obligations, we will explain why.

7. Updates and contact

Updates to this policy will be presented in the App. For questions, please contact your enterprise administrator or data protection contact.
